Progression: make a license with your name.
  Each one keeps its own unlocks and times.
Unlock characters - karts - bikes - cups
  and Mirror by racing - like on the Wii.
Want it all now: turn on Unlock Everything
  in Settings - the button in the LiveArea.
Settings: presets - resolution - graphics.
Expert staff ghost times unlock rewards.
Battle: random teams - thicker map outlines.
Coin Runners: hits drop coins to grab - coins
  on the minimap - half the starting coins.
